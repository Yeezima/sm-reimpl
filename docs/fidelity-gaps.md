# Fidelity gaps: what the original MP had that we don't yet (audit 2026-10-28)

lists, `util.oppc` constant pools by name only) against our implementation, ranked by how noticeable each gap is. Scope: the
alpha (Manufactorum, Annihilation and Seize Ground). Notes and extracts: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\sm-reimpl\cache\fidelity-audit\`
(private). S < 1 day, M a few days, L a week or more.
