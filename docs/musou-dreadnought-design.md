# Musou Dreadnought: design (2026-11-14)

Status: **design only**; nothing is built. Owner's request (2026-11-14): "Add a new class to Musou mode - next to librarian and heretic selection options in the customize UI - dreadnaught" and "The dreadnaught gameplay matches exactly that of the loyalist space marine dreadnaught. Use the original game data and assets to match exactly the gameplay and visual experience of the dreadnaught from the dreadnaught assault game mode."

The original's data is in `docs/discovery.md` "Dreadnought (Dreadnought Assault)" (every number below that is not tagged comes from there; kinds are spec-style: **data** = read from the install, **estimate**, **guess**, **ours**). The class is added content (`ContentExtras.Musou`): it lives in `Extras/Musou` folders and nothing in Classic PvP (Annihilation, Seize Ground) changes (`docs/added-content.md`, `MusouIsolationTests`).

## 1. What the original gives us, in one paragraph

An 8,000-health, 11 m/s sprinting walker with no armour shield and 250 health a second of regeneration after 5 s, that cannot be staggered or moved (its hit reactions all map to short flinches; the buff removes even those), and that has five tools: an assault cannon (30 damage, 0.06 s a round, 140-round clip, 2.5 s reload, scatter and climb tables), a melta (200 damage, one round a clip, 0.33 s cooldown), an arm with a five-strike melee set (100/125/50/stomp/500 on a sprint, with area slams of 500 and 50 and a foot-stomp of 25 on every footfall), a sprint ability (6 s, 1 s gap, 50 degrees a second turning) and a 10 s buff (`fury_mode` plus reaction immunity plus double regeneration, once). It enters with 5 s of invulnerability after a 4.8 s entrance animation and is healed 1,500 by a control point. All of it is on a real rig with 155 animations, a model, textures, an own sound bank and own cameras. What is **not** in the data we read: the mode's spawn and respawn rules for the unit (not needed, see section 9 question 1), the camera offset curves `dn_*`, the heat table `heat_autocannon`, the model's world size, and the capsule.

## 3. Simulation rules (all in `src/SmSim/Extras/Musou/Dreadnought/`, behind existing hooks)

Pattern: exactly the Librarian's. `World.Dreadnought.cs` holds `Dictionary<int, DreadnoughtState>` (cooldowns, buff timer, entry timer, sprint timer, melta clip, stomp phase), fed through the same `ExtrasSampled` input hook and stepped from the same Musou step; **nothing goes into `PlayerState`** except what already exists there (class, health, Clip, Reserve, Heat, Slot, MeleeStep, Rush). The extras travel on a new Musou envelope tag (the next free one above 18; confirm in `TfwClientState`).

### 3.1 Movement and body (data unless noted)

- Speeds m/s: run 6.5, strafe 6.5, back 4.5, sprint 11, walk 3; aiming run 5, strafe 5, back 4; crouch values (5/4/4/9/3.6) are in the body but **crouch is disabled** for him (the data has no crouch clip: **guess**).
- Acceleration: `ground_time_till_max_speed` 0.6 s to full speed (the marine config's own rule is replaced by it for this body: needs one `BodySpec` field, or the Extras step scales the velocity; decided in D1 by what the core accepts).
- Turning: legs up to 360 degrees a second (data), but **aim yaw 60 degrees a second** (`aim_horizontal_speed`, data) while idle-turn clips play past 75 degrees of offset; this is the "tank" feel of the original and is applied to the view's effect on the body, not to the mouse (the mouse stays free: the data's `speed_mouse` 50 / 25 aimed are camera speeds). **Estimate** (needs a playtest): how the original maps mouse input to the 60 degrees a second; first pass turns the body toward the view at 360 and lets the torso lead.
- No jump, no evade, no mantle; falling lands with `DropLandLight/Heavy` (data: `FLT_Fall_Speed` below -20 is heavy). Max slope 60 (data).
- **Collision size: estimate.** The data gives `unit_size` really_big and a melee hit volume of radius 1.8 m, length 2.3 m, but no capsule. The core capsule, step height and eye height are the config's for every class (`Bodies.cs` header). First pass: keep the marine capsule for walking (so Ashfall's graph and doors hold; **estimate**), give the Dreadnought **Ork-side clearance**: the crowd's push ring and the player-solid rule for him use radius 1.5 m (`MusouSim.Solids` / players' ring, **guess**) and his hit volumes are scaled to the model (below). A larger movement capsule needs one core hook or field (question 3).
- Pushing: Orks never move him (`MusouSolids`: players are not pushed; his data says the same: push only becomes a flinch). Big Orks (Warboss, Boss Orks) are solid to him as to any player (`MusouSolids.Blocks`).
- Footstep stomps: each footfall (`evt_spawn_r_stomp`/`_l_`; the clip's footfall tags, from `DN_NAV_Walk/Run_*`) damages everything within **4.5 m** (D0 correction 2026-11-14: the data's damage area is a **10 m** sphere, `area_of_effect/shape`; 4.5 m is only the debris-force sphere; spec "Dreadnought: the areas"): 25 melee damage, an upward kick of 60 degrees and 50 m/s at most and the stagger table (0.8 stagger 4 m, 0.61 stun 3 m, else 2 m), stun damage 1000. In Musou: ordinary Orks are thrown/staggered by the existing Ork reaction (as a melee blast); elites and bosses take the damage only (they are stagger-immune by the Musou rules, `LibrarianRules.TierOf`). **Estimate**: whether the original's stomp fires on every footfall in motion, or only in the stomp attack and the sprint; the blueprint has the two events, the animation tags that call them were not decoded (D5 reads them from the clips). Each footfall hit counts as a melee hit event for kill credit.

### 3.2 Health, regeneration, damage taken

- Health 8000, no shield, regeneration delay 5 s, rate **250/s** (data, `BodySpec`); the buff doubles the rate (data).
- Entrance: on class pick or respawn, 5 s with **all received damage multiplied by 0** (data `evt_spawn_in`), while the 4.8 s entrance clip plays and he cannot move or fire until it ends (**guess**: the data's Spawn Entry state ends by `BRANCH_SpawnEnd`; the clip is 4.833 s). The Librarian's instant class change does not apply; a respawn uses the same entrance.
- **Balance problem, the largest in this design.** Musou's Orks deal 8 (Boy, every 1.4 s), 10, 25 (Nob), 60 times the danger scale (Warboss), 120 (Rokkit) against a marine of about 100 health plus shield. Against 8,000 health and 250 a second of regeneration, an ordinary crowd cannot hurt him: 30 Boys swinging deal about 170 damage a second, below the regeneration. Taken literally the data makes the class **immortal in Musou** against everything but long Warboss and rocket fire. This is a gap to settle with the owner before D8 (question 2); the design carries one knob so it is a data decision and not a rewrite: `DreadnoughtRules.IncomingScale` (applied in `World.MusouHit` beside `PlayerDamageScale`; **default 1.0 = the data**; a Musou-balance candidate is 8 to 20, **guess**, so that he dies to a sustained crowd as a marine does in proportion). The bosses' per-hit cap (`MusouBossRules.MaxHitShare`) already reads the body's health, so it scales with 8000 by itself (it would let a boss hit take up to 6,000: also to review).
- Reactions: no stagger, knockback or stun from any Ork (data; and Musou already denies it, `EnemiesImpede`). The flinch clips play on large hits (visual only); no state change. The buff's `mp_reaction_immunity` then hides the flinches too.
- Death: 10 s `death_time`, `DN_REC_Death_01` (3.433 s), `thunderhawk_engine_explosion` at `fx_l_shoulder`, `play_or_horde_bodyfall`; no ragdoll. Lives pool and respawn: the Musou shared lives pool as any player (no special cost: **guess**; the original's respawn is the mode's).

### 3.3 Weapons

Read by a new `DreadnoughtData` reader in `src/SmContent/Extras/Musou/` (D0), like `OrkClass.Read`: every number below comes from the wargear, and `DreadnoughtRules` holds only names and the tags the reader does not find. Install-gated parity tests pin each number.

- **Assault cannon** (Fire, held): hit-scan, 30 damage per round (ranged, strong), 0.06 s apart (1000 rpm), infinite reserve, 140 round clip, low-ammo cue at 15, auto-reload at empty or on Reload, 2.5 s reload (the 3 s animation is blended to it, data), scatter and climb from `sm_mp_dreadnought_assaultcannon` (hip) and `_aim` (Aim held), muzzle `fx_muzzle_assault_cannon`. Range 250 m (data); against Musou Orks the existing Musou ranged rules apply (`MusouGunBurst`, ranged scale, no range fall-off in the war, endless clip rules: his clip is his own 140 and is **kept** as the data's reload is part of his rhythm; the Musou "endless clip" rule is for the autocannon only; **estimate** whether to keep it, question 4). Heat: 0.5 a shot into `heat_autocannon` (table unread); with 140 rounds at 0.06 s a clip lasts 8.4 s; **guess**: no overheat in the first pass.
- **Melta** (the Switch key swaps cannon and melta as a primary/pistol toggle; the weapon box shows it): one hit-scan round, 200 damage (and the `is_in_coop` 100 variant: **estimate**, first pass 200), 0.33 s cooldown (0.095 s during the buff), clip 1 reloading at 1 round a second after 0.33 s, `play_melta_gun_pump` between shots, hit container `meltagun`. In Musou: a single target hit-scan; piercing along the beam to cut through ranks is **not** in the data: not added (the owner can ask).
- **Arm (melee)**: the five strikes as the graph's combos (section 3.4), input mapped as the existing melee buttons: Melee = X, MeleeHeavy = Y (the sim already has both for every marine melee weapon, `InputButtons`), Sprint + Melee = the rush. Damage 100 primary and 50 secondary (X1), 125 and 75 (X2), 50 (Y1), 500 and 125 (rush), the slam 500 and the stomp 50 in a 10 m sphere (data; not 4.5 m, D0 correction), each with armour damage from the data and stun damage 150 or 1000 (stun damage only matters for Ork armour and the Weirdboy's shield). Reach 4 m, ideal 1 to 3 m (data), the lock-on `auto_aim_angle` x3 for 2 s. In Musou the cleave: his sweeps use the Musou cleave shapes (`MusouCleaveShape`) with the arm's 4 m reach as the radius, not the hit phantom (1.8 m x 2.3 m), **estimate** (question 5); the player cleave scale (`PlayerCleaveReachScale`) is **not** applied to him: his reach is the data's.
- **Sprint**: Sprint input held: 11 m/s for at most 6 s, then a 1 s gap (data); while sprinting his turn rate is capped at 50 degrees a second (data); a wall hit within 20 degrees ends it with `DN_NAV_Bullrush_F_Collision` (2.467 s recovery, `prop_smash`). Sprint + Melee = `melee_x_rush`. Against the crowd the sprint has **no body damage** (the data's collision actions are empty); **guess** that the original's sprint pushed small units aside by its push response only: in Musou Orks stay solid to him except fodder (the existing `MusouSolids.Blocks` rule), so the sprint is a way to cover ground, and the rush is the weapon.
- **Buff** (the equipment key, `Grenade`): 10 s of `fury_mode` and reaction immunity, regeneration x2, cooldown 10 s, one use per life (clip 1, no reserve: data). Implemented as the marine stim's mechanism (`StimSpec` with `ThrownSpec.NoProjectile`; no new status system) with the stim's Musou scaling **off** (the Musou combat stims' x3 lifetime and melee x2 are the marines' rules; his buff is the data's: **estimate**, question 6). `fury_mode`'s own effect is not in the data we read: first pass applies the melta's fire rate change (data) and the double regeneration only.
- **Control point heal**: 1,500 health when he captures a Musou capture point (`evt_dread_cap`; the Musou war has capture points: hook on the existing capture event; **estimate** that it should fire per point, not per capture tick).
- Not carried: grenades, pistols, perks, jump pack, evade, the Musou weapon drops (`MusouDropItem` gives are refused for him: his class has no weapon slots they could fill; a melee drop is refused as there is one arm), the Musou support abilities (decision pending, question 7).

### 3.4 Melee combo table (from the graph; sim state machine in `DreadnoughtMelee.cs`)

```
none --X--> X01 --X--> X02 --X--> X01
none --Y--> STOMP        X01/X02 --Y--> Y01 --Y--> STOMP   Y01 --X--> X01
stick > 135 degrees behind: the _180_ clip of the same strike (the strike turns the body first)
Sprint + X: X_RUSH (500)         Sprint pressed in a strike: leaves it (Melee_Sprint)
```

Timings (clip length, hit moment) come from the clips' tags (`graph-dump ... --clips DN_MEL` + `--root-motion`), read by D0 into `DreadnoughtMelee.Steps` as `MeleeStep`s (the same record the marines' combos use) so the core's step machinery (`MeleeStep`, `MeleeQueued`) and the client's strike animation selection work unchanged where possible. Open in the data: the strike lengths are long (2.3 to 4.2 s with the recovery) and the original's X attacks chain only after the clip's cancel window (`BRANCH_MeleeCombo`); the window is a tag, read in D0.

## 4. Orks, bosses and the war

- **Ork targeting**: Musou Orks choose targets by `MusouTargetRules` (kind and distance); the Dreadnought is a player target like any: no rule change. A taunt or priority for him is **not** designed (an option: `unit_size really_big` could raise the Orks' target weight; **guess**, question 8).
- **Rockets and the Rokkit Boy**: 120 damage a round: 1.5 % of his health; the incoming-rocket HUD arc still warns him.
- **Bosses**: the Warboss's slam (60 x danger scale) and charge knock marines down; for him no reaction (data) but the damage lands; his melee stun (rush and slams carry 150 and 1000 stun damage) interacts with the existing boss rules: **bosses stay unstunnable except by a player's melee stagger** (owner, 2026-11-12, `MusouSim.BossStun.cs`): his X strikes call the same melee stagger as any marine's heavy melee (**estimate**: the original's `th_x` reaction on the target is a knockdown-class reaction; here it is the Musou melee stagger rule); the 500 slam must not one-hit a boss: it deals damage 500 against 15,000 health.
- **The Librarian and AI Marines** (the war's allies): he is a player-class target for allied logic as any; the Guard squads' hit rules (`TfwGuardHit`) are for the Guards, no change.
- **The capture points and the war flow** are class-blind; the entrance and the lives pool are the only class-specific parts.
- **Stomps and friendly fire**: Musou has no player-player damage; the stomps and slams never hurt Marines or Guards (**ours**, as every Musou blast).

## 5. Camera, aim and input

- Third person, the marine camera code (`CameraRig`) driven by the Dreadnought's **data**: field of view 42 (explore), 20 (aim), 55 (sprint), 35 (melee hit); blend times 0.25 / 0.15 / 0.5 / 0.33 s; pitch limits 20 to 160, 5 to 175, 50 to 120; look speeds (free 150/125, aimed 40/25, mouse 50 / 25). **Gap**: the camera offset curves `dn_*` were not found, so the distance, height and shoulder offset are **guesses** (the marine's, scaled by the model's size ratio ~ x2; owner's reference images or videos requested, question 9). The aimed camera zooms to a narrow 20 degrees: the existing Musou aim key works (Aim, held).
- Crosshair: the autocannon reticle for the cannon, the melta reticle for the melta; the bolter reticle for melee (data names; their pictures exist for marines). Aim assist: `dlc3_dreadnought` free and `_aim` aimed, loaded through the existing aim-assist reader if the client has one for Musou (else none in the first pass; **estimate** the effect for mouse play is small).
- Input mapping (existing bits, no new input bit): Fire (cannon/melta), Aim, Reload (cannon only), Switch (cannon <-> melta), Melee (X), MeleeHeavy (Y), Sprint, Grenade (the buff, as the Librarian's equipment key), Jump/crouch unused.

- **Model and rig**: new `RigProfiles.Dreadnought` (archive `data/chunks/ebp_sets__mp_versus_set.oppc`, packfile `havok_sm_dreadnought_basic__hkxx`) like `RigProfiles.OrkBoy`; the model `sm_dreadnought_basic.dcm` with the `Dreadnought_*.bmat` materials and the `sm_dread_basic_*` textures through the existing DCM, BMAT and DDS loaders (the Warboss and Stormboy models use the same path). *Original asset loaded* at runtime, never in the repo. The readers worked for the animation side (`graph-dump` parses the file, 155 animations, 54 bones); the `.dcm` read is **untested** (D2 first step: load it in the model viewer, `ModelViewer`).
- **Locomotion**: the graph's 8-way strafe at idle/walk/run speeds (2 and 4.5 to 6.5) with aim up/forward/down variants, idle turns past 75 degrees, drop landings; the client already has an Ork rig animation player (`CharacterViewer` family) driven by the graph's variables (`FLT_Input_Nav_Speed_DAMPED`, `FLT_Nav_Motion_Angle_DAMPED`, `FLT_Aim_Horizontal_Deg_DAMPED`, `FLT_Aim_Vertical_Deg_DAMPED`, `BOOL_Melta`, `BOOL_SprintIsActive`, `BTN_*` events): D2 adds `DreadnoughtLook` mapping the sim's state onto them; if the full graph evaluation is out of reach, a clip-picking fallback uses the named clips (`DN_NAV_Walk_F_AimF_01`, ...) as `CharacterViewer.Layers` does for the Boys (**estimate**: the graph evaluation is the same Havok behaviour reader `graph-dump` uses, so it should be available).
- **Weapons on the body**: the cannon and the arm are part of the model (`sm_dread_basic_assaultcannon`, `_meleearm`); the melta is the left arm in the `LAim` clips (a state of the same model, no separate mesh).
- **VFX** (named, from the data; `FxPlayer` plays `master_fx` effects): `autocannon_muzzle` and `shell_casing` per round, `bolter_smoke` on stop, `chaos_kraken_trail` and `dlc3_dread_assault_cannon` hit effects, the melta's cylinder blast (`meltagun_cylinder_blast_*`), `thunderhammer_antic/swing/dmg_displace_heavy`, `bullrush_swing`, `interrupt_kick_grnd_swing`, `knockback_stomp_dmg_furied` and `interrupt_stomp_dmg` for the stomp, `jump_land` dust per footfall, `jumppack_land_heavy` on landing, `powersource_explode`, `amb_chaos_portal_clouds`, `powersource_glow`, `plasma_gun_high_heat`, `inq_weapon_center_lightning`, `fury_activated_titus_start`, `iron_halo_break_shoulders`, `perkfx_reactiveblast_exp`, `thunderhawk_engine_explosion` and the engine lights.
- **Camera effects**: the footfall, stomp and cannon shakes (data amplitudes) through the client's existing camera-effect support (Musou has the Warboss slam and the Librarian shockwave shake); the stomp's 0.3 s blur 0.25 (data).

## 9. Version and boundary impact

- **Classic: none.** No core file changes except at most: (a) one `BodySpec` field for acceleration and capsule if question 3 says so (hashed only when the body exists: Classic configs have no bodies), (b) hooks already in place (`ExtrasSampled`, `ExtrasRecordBodies`, `ExtrasTopLevel` and friends); every new hook is pinned in `ExtrasBoundaryTests` as the rule requires.
- **`MusouSim.Version`** +1 (56 at the time of writing, at integration), **`TfwRules.Version`** +1 (81), both because the class table (hashed under Musou) changes and a new wire tag appears. **`Protocol.Version`** and `ContentExtras.WireVersion` unchanged (the Librarian's tag 16 precedent). The config hash changes under Musou only; a server and client of different builds refuse each other as before.
- **Wire**: one new envelope tag for the Dreadnought's extras (cooldowns, buff, entry, sprint, melta clip, melee step, footstep stomp events): about 12 bytes a Dreadnought per send; the server sends it only while one is in the match (as tag 16).
- **Save formats**: no profile change (no loadout to save).
- **Restore switches** (the usual): `SM_DREADNOUGHT_OLD` is not needed on day one (nothing precedes the class); every tunable gets a named constant and a spec row.
- **Spec and register**: a new heading "Added content: Musou Dreadnought" in `spec/behavior.md` with one row per number (kind, source, confidence), rows in `docs/changes-from-original.md` section 0 for every "ours" rule (section 3's estimates), then `smtool refine-register`.

## 12. Questions the owner must answer

1. **Is he a pick by anyone, any number at a time** (first pass) or one per match as in Dreadnought Assault (the mode's rule, not read)? My default: any number.
2. **Damage he takes.** The data's 8,000 health and 250 regeneration a second make ordinary Orks harmless to him (section 3.2). Choose: (a) exactly the data (he is a power fantasy; only bosses and rockets matter), (b) Orks hit him x8 to x20 so a crowd can wear him down (my suggestion, a guess to tune), (c) a Musou health of his own (say 1,500 to 3,000) with the data's regeneration. This is the largest decision.
3. **His size in the world.** Keep the marine's walking capsule (doors and stairs work; he looks a giant but walks like a marine) or give him a true 2 to 3 m body (needs a small core change, Ashfall pathing check)? Also, are the Orks to treat him as a "big body" like Boss Orks (solid, waded through)?
4. **Cannon clip.** The Musou rules give the autocannon an endless clip; the Dreadnought's data has a 140-round clip with a 2.5 s reload. Keep the clip and reload as in the data (my default)?
5. **Melee speed and reach in a horde.** The data's strikes run 2.3 to 4.2 s with the recovery; Musou's marines got attack speed x1.2 and a 20 percent reach bonus. Keep his strikes exactly as the data (default) or apply the Musou melee scales?
6. **The buff.** The data's buff is a 10 s stim of its own, once per life. Keep that, or give it the Musou stim rules (three times the length, double melee damage)? And should it recharge (data: no)?
7. **Support abilities** (artillery, drop pod): does the Dreadnought get the Musou support pick like the Librarian, or not?
8. **Target weight.** Should the Orks prefer to attack him (he is huge and loud) or treat him as any player?
9. **Camera and feel references**: a video or screenshots of the Dreadnought's camera in Dreadnought Assault (distance, height, the turn rate of the legs), since the offset curves are not readable; also any recollection of the stomp (every footfall or only the attack), and whether the melta pierced.
10. **Menu order and name**: the entry after Heretic (as asked) labelled "Dreadnought" (the game's spelling; the request's "dreadnaught" is the game's own file spelling for the picture and the script): confirm.
