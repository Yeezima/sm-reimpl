# Playtest build 264e336c (2026-11-08)

Protocol 64. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-11-08-264e336

- Musou Mode: every battle on Ashfall now starts at the Imperial base in the south centre of the map, and redeploying after death or joining a battle in progress brings you there too (before, most battles dropped you somewhere random on the map). Everyone must update: this build does not play with earlier ones.
- Musou Mode: Orks taking back a point now spread out across it instead of piling up in the middle, and Orks standing guard look around (and at you when you come near) instead of all staring the same way.
- Musou Mode: only two allied Space Marines fight beside you now, however many players there are. They land with you and roam the map together, pushing the point being attacked and running to defend a held point the Orks attack; a fallen one is back at the landing zone after 30 seconds. Your personal retainer, the Brother-Captains and the six Defenders are gone, and Z, X and V no longer give orders (X and V are back to next weapon and melee).
- Musou Mode: the bolt weapons' explosions are weaker: half the reach, half the damage and a smaller flash. An Ork they kill is torn apart where he stands instead of flying away, and one they hurt is knocked to the ground. Testers must all update (new game version).
- Hosting on your own PC (Musou Mode or a local lobby) no longer stutters when the PC is busy: the server you host keeps its steady pace, so Orks, allies and your own Marine stop jumping back and forth.
- Musou Mode, Ashfall: the floor no longer shimmers with FSR 2 upscaling on, and the dirt on floors and walls no longer flickers in patches as you move.
