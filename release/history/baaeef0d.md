# Playtest build baaeef0d (2026-11-10)

Protocol 65. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-11-10-baaeef0

- New network version: everyone needs this build, in Annihilation, Seize Ground and Musou Mode alike. Older builds and this one can no longer play together, and the public server is updated with it.

### Everyone
- The game reacts faster: your own moves and shots show sooner, other players move more smoothly, and the hit marker and hit sound come on the frame you fire.
- Loading screen: a picture behind LOADING, and a short fade to black between the menu, the loading screen and the match.
- The Stalker Bolter and the Daemon Eye play their own hit sound when you hit someone.
- Plasma gun and plasma pistol shots look like the original game's.
- GRAPHICS options fit the window again: no scroll bar, and the note and BACK show below the list.
- Please tell us if a player snaps back a little right after a bull rush or charge hits: the faster updates are new and that moment is the one we are watching.

### Musou Mode
- The Orks are back to full numbers, Nobs and Big Shoota Nobs come in pairs, and up to 8 Boss Orks can be alive at once, arriving sooner.
- Orks run faster and sprint at you from about 30 m away. Big Orks fall with a proper death, walk at a natural pace, climb stairs and no longer snag on walls.
- Guardsmen are tougher and hit harder, most of all against charging Orks.
- The Weirdboy casts his lightning bolt again.
- Combat stims recharge on their own like grenades: one back every 15 seconds.
- The screen is clearer: the Musou displays sit at the edges and corners, and your armour and health are at the bottom left with the boss health bars above them, the bosses you are hitting first.
- Capture points show their owner with a banner in the middle: red while the Orks hold it, blue once your side does. A burst of smoke covers it when it changes hands, and a black circle on the ground fills as a point is captured. The letters above the points fade out as you get close.
- Press T for battle cries: "For the Emperor" and six more lines in turn. The automatic voice lines are gone.
- Red and blue slash strokes are no longer drawn farther than 30 m away.
- Exploding bolter rounds no longer make the grenade explosion sound, so heavy fights are far less drowned out.
- The loadout screen has its own Musou choices: Standard Marines, the Librarian (his powers and look, and Play as Librarian in a match) and the Heretic (coming later).
- Shooting through a shield dome now hits its generator.
- Ashfall's buildings are sharper on normal graphics; the old-graphics-card mode uses lighter textures and much less video memory.
