# Playtest build da1f5b88 (2026-11-09)

Protocol 64. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-11-09-da1f5b8

- This build's game settings differ from the last one, so everyone must update together.

### Musou Mode
- Ashfall is rebuilt as a city: wide avenues link the landing zone, the Ork side and the three points, buildings stand in rows along the streets, and every staircase can be walked up from the street.
- Armies of Orks and Guardsmen now march along the avenues instead of squeezing through narrow alleys, so the big clashes happen on wide fronts.
- Melee cleave reaches a little less far than in the last build (1.3x the normal reach instead of 1.5x).
- Every Scrap Mek contraption can now be destroyed: shield generators, beacons, Rokkit turrets and scrap traps all go down after a few bolter rounds or one melee blow, and the shield dome no longer protects its own generator.
