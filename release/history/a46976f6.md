# Playtest build a46976f6 (2026-11-15)

Protocol 79. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-11-15-a46976f

- This build has a new network version: everyone must update, and older builds cannot join its servers.
### Classic PvP
- New maps: every other competitive map from the original is now playable in Annihilation and Seize Ground, among them Shattered Bridge, Waste Management, Basilica, Hab Center, Habs Ablaze, Station Tertius, Chem Refinery, Aquila Canyon, Desolation and Dome Mechanicus. Shattered Bridge is the most tested; the others may still have rough spots, so please report anything odd.
- Hosting: the Host panel has a MAP choice, and a dedicated server can rotate through several maps, one match each.
- Sprinting with Shift + W, right-click now charges instead of swinging, for every class with a charge, the Ork Boy included. Let go of Shift (keep W) and right-click swings at once.
- Lascannon: tap reload to load one round in 0.4 s (you cannot fire during that 0.4 s). Holding the zoom while walking no longer bobs the crosshair.
### Classic PvP and Musou Mode
- After charging off a ledge, a light attack at any moment in the air stops the charge's forward glide and swings in the direction you hold or look.
### Musou Mode
- The Dreadnought is playable: pick it in the customizer. It starts in the middle of the Imperial base and comes back there when it redeploys; several Dreadnoughts stand a few metres apart.
