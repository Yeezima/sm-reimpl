# Playtest build e792504 (2026-10-29)

Protocol 39. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-10-29-e792504

- The charge no longer needs a sprint: hold W and press F. Hold A or D as well to charge a little to that side.
- Assault and Raptor: the dash attack (right mouse during the hop-dash) now goes in eight directions. Hold W, A, S, D or two of them when you press right mouse; with no key it follows your view.
- The main menu now fits the window: the JOIN, QUIT and CUSTOMIZE buttons are no longer cut off at the bottom. When the window is short, the middle of the menu scrolls instead.
- Hosting a match on this PC now adds 7 bots, so you get a 4v4 right away. Choose No bots, 4v4 or 8v8 and Easy or Normal under BOTS before you host, or press + BOT and - BOT while it runs. The "Host a match on this PC" launcher starts the same 7 bots. Each bot uses about 0.65 GB of memory.
- STOP SERVER in the HOST panel stops the server you hosted and its bots, or one started by the "Host a match on this PC" launcher.
- Joining no longer waits forever: if nothing answers within 10 seconds, the game says "No answer from ..." and Esc takes you back to the menu. A server that refuses you now says why in plain words.
- If the port you host on is already taken (for example by an older server still running), the menu says so and does not join it.
- The "NEXT MAP - 0:00" line no longer shows while you are still connecting.
- This build does not play with earlier builds: everyone in a match needs this version.
