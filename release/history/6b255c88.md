# Playtest build 6b255c88 (2026-11-20)

Protocol 88. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-11-20-6b255c8

- Warfront: a retainer's jump pack (Assault and Raptor squads) is now painted like the backpack colours you picked in the customizer, instead of the side's default paint.
- Warfront: your Marine retainers now shoot like the practice bots but miss far less, stick to the Ork they are fighting for about a second instead of flipping targets, favour Orks that attack you or a downed squad-mate, and grow tougher and hit harder as your level rises. Everyone must update: Warfront games from older builds will not match.
- Mapmaker: Play test no longer refuses a map that holds an original sky piece (for example seq_07_sky from an imported original map). The sky is still drawn, you just cannot collide with it.
- Mapmaker: the Map tab has a new Grid section (below Snap). Tick Show grid, pick the grid's colour, set the cell size in metres and the height (or press From selection to put it at the bottom of the selected object), and tick Draw over objects to see the grid through pieces. The grid never changes when you zoom, is saved with your map and does not change how the map plays.
- Mapmaker: importing an original map now brings Classic's invisible walls, kill boxes, shoot-through cover and the objects Classic hides (switched off, you can turn them back on), and sets the original look for the map.
- Mapmaker: new pieces in the Build tab, Invisible wall (players only) and Cover (shoot-through), drawn see-through (teal and orange) in the editor; select a wall and pick Box or Cylinder on the Object tab. In Map > Look, pick an original map's lighting and sky for your map (players need that map installed to join). Map > Refresh from Classic lists what Classic added, removed or changed since you imported a map; tick what to take over and press Apply selected, or Apply all I haven't edited (one Undo takes it back). In the Scene tab, objects Classic hides show greyed, and a tick box lists only those; the import message now counts walls, kill boxes, cover and hidden objects. The Undo button in the Mapmaker panel works again.
- Warfront: a new ONLINE WARFRONT row on the Warfront page joins the project's shared online campaign (up to 4 players drop in and out), with nothing to type.
- Warfront: twice as many Guardsmen fight in every battle, standing in rows around the points they hold. They are a little weaker, so the Orks win about two of every three even fights.
- Warfront: the war map, travel and results screens sit in the same frame as the rest of the menus, and the results show your own, your squad's and the total score under their own headings.
- Power sword: after the T stab you can move, shoot, roll or swing again as soon as the thrust has hit or missed (about 0.9 s instead of 1.9 s). In Warfront the finisher still follows the stab if you press it in time.
- Dreadnought (Warfront): the charge and melee play smoothly instead of stuttering.
- Armoury: your class emblems are new; CONFIRM saves the class, makes it active and takes you back to the classes; DEPLOY appears only in a match; Rename, Copy, Reset and Clear sit in the Slots tab; leaving with unsaved changes asks Keep or Discard in a proper window. Devastator and Havoc no longer show a melee card they cannot change, and fixed slots show a padlock.
- Menus: sharper buttons and text with even margins; long descriptions now appear as a hint when you hold the mouse over an option. The No Perk choice has its own icon.
- Main menu: the background video is sharper and centred on the Marine, the menu is smaller in the bottom-left corner, and the address line at the bottom is gone.
- Everyone must update: this build does not play with earlier ones.
