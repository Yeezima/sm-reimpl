# Playtest build 22cd46be (2026-11-10)

Protocol 65. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-11-10-22cd46b

- Musou Mode has loadouts of its own now, kept apart from your Classic loadouts: the first time you open Musou's customizer they start as a copy of your Classic ones. The Librarian gets his own loadout too: melee weapon, equipment, two perks and a look. Opening an older build of the game can reset your Musou loadouts.
- Fewer stacked sounds in big fights: the game's own limits on how many copies of one sound play at once now apply to every sound (before, only to footsteps), so bolter fire, hits and blasts no longer pile up on themselves. Please report any hit, kill or voice sound that seems to go missing.
- Press F3 in a match for a performance readout in the top right corner (off by default; F3 again adds the network: your ping, snapshot loss, interpolation delay and input slack; a third press hides it). Please have it on when you report stutter or lag.
- In old GPU / safe mode Musou Mode's Ashfall always uses the FAST grime look (the Compatibility renderer paid most for the extra pass).
- Musou Mode: a new piece of equipment, the Remote Detonators (pick them in the customizer's equipment row, press the grenade key to throw). A charge sticks to walls, the floor and Orks, even big ones, and goes off five seconds later: a huge blast, then a ring of explosions rolling round it that throws Orks into the air. You get one, and a new one every 30 seconds. This build does not play with earlier ones.
- Musou Mode: the Devastator can take an autocannon (last in his heavy weapons): it fires slower than the heavy bolter but hits harder, and every round bursts in a small explosion. Its muzzle flash, tracer and sound are stand-ins for now.
- Musou Mode: two new melee weapons for the Assault Marine, in the customizer's melee row. The Lightning Claw: one claw on the right hand with a blue power glow and the bolt pistol, faster but lighter blows than the power sword. The Power Spear: slower, heavier blows that sweep a wide arc of Orks in front of you, and its slam from the air impales an Ork. For now the claw moves like the power sword and the spear like the thunder hammer.
- Musou Mode's Librarian: a new cast pose with his hands thrust towards the target, a stronger chain lightning that strikes the moment you click, a Shockwave that reaches 60 m and hits much harder, and no more Warp Pull, so he can take any equipment the other classes can.
- Musou Mode feels heavier: feet and landings kick up dust and make contact sounds, bodies settle after a move, blows land with more punch, stances blend into each other, and big blasts shake the view a little less.
- Musou Mode draws blood sprays and other spray effects on the graphics card, which should keep big fights smoother.
