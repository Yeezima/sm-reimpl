# Playtest build 397b519 (2026-10-29)

Protocol 40. Release: https://github.com/Yeezima/sm-reimpl-releases/releases/tag/tester-2026-10-29-397b519

- Combat Stimulants are now in the equipment choices: press G to inject them. For 5 seconds you take less damage to your health (half from melee), hit harder in melee and are not knocked back or stunned by hits. Favor of the Armory gives you two; Master-Crafted Wargear makes them last 7 seconds and work better. Everyone must play the same build (servers refuse an older one).
- Shots, explosions, the ground pound and the map no longer fill the screen with smoke and dust: impacts throw a few pieces of debris with their flash and sparks and leave a mark on the wall, explosions keep their fireball and flash. Floor markings no longer show crisp through clouds and fire in front of them.
- Smoother, faster game: the game's own code now runs fully optimised (it was not before), so frames take about a third less time and the camera, aiming and effects cost much less.
- Bots you add when hosting are much lighter: each needs about 0.27 GB of memory instead of 0.65 GB and a small fraction of the processor time it did, because it no longer loads the graphics, effects and sounds nobody sees or hears and runs at 30 frames a second, so your own game keeps its frame rate. On a PC with 8 GB, about 4 bots is a safe number.
- Fewer stutters: the hitch when the first ammunition drop appeared (about 20 seconds into every match) and the stutter while aiming at a jump-pack player in flight are gone.
- New: "0 - SM Reimpl Launcher.exe" at the top of the zip. Run the launcher; it keeps you up to date: it installs the build into its own folder, looks for a new build each time it starts, downloads it (with progress, resume after a broken connection and automatic retries), checks every file before switching, keeps the build before it for "Roll back", and starts PLAY, Host and Join. Your profile and settings are never touched. Windows may warn once ("More info" > "Run anyway"): the launcher is not code-signed. The zip's other files work as before.
