- New network version: this build does not play with earlier ones, so everyone must update together.
- No shooting while sprinting, with any weapon: while you sprint (Shift and W) the trigger does nothing, the lascannon included. A charge you were holding is lost when you start to sprint, and letting go of the trigger mid-sprint fires nothing. Let go of Shift to shoot: the gun comes up as it always did after a sprint (the heavy weapons, melta and grenade launcher at once); single-shot and charge weapons need a fresh click. Holding the trigger no longer slows your sprint.
- Devastator and Havoc: the stomp can no longer be cut short by shooting, aiming, melee or any other button; only a hop (Space) cancels it, from the same moment as before.
- Devastator and Havoc: running or sprinting off right after a stomp no longer leaves the arms stuck in the stomp pose over running legs; the arms now go into the run or sprint as soon as you can move. The same goes for the knife strikes and the heavy classes' kick.
- Spent bolt casings drop to the ground much faster instead of floating.
- Storm Bolter (Tactical) and the Chaos Twin Bolter: every shot now uses its rounds as in the original, so a full magazine lasts 5 seconds of full-auto fire instead of 10 and you reload twice as often. The ammo counter drops by two with each shot (one round per barrel).
- Controller: the B button now closes the open menu or goes back one level, like Esc (the in-match menu, loadouts, armour and colours, controls, dialogs, and the connecting screen). While a controls entry waits for a new button, B is bound as usual. In a match B still uses and picks up, and the B that closes a menu no longer also picks something up.
- Tactical and Chaos Tactical: no shooting during the roll, with any weapon. Holding or pressing the trigger no longer cuts the roll short with a shot; the roll plays out, and you can shoot once it ends (or once you break out of it by moving, aiming or throwing a grenade, from the same moment as before). A charge you were holding is lost when you roll; single-shot and charge weapons need a fresh click after the roll. The Devastator and Havoc hop and the jump-pack dash are unchanged.
- Every class can now look and shoot straight up into the sky, in hip fire and while aiming or zoomed; before, the view stopped well short of vertical. Looking down is unchanged.
- The stalker bolter's and lascannon's scope now darkens the whole screen on a wide window too: the strips left and right of the picture (any window wider than 16:9, such as 2000 x 1070 or ultrawide) were lighter than the scope's tint and are now the same tint, edge to edge. The reticle stays centred and round.
- Devastator and Havoc: the hop now goes down stairs and off ledges while it keeps going forward, instead of floating at the height you started from and then dropping straight down.
- Assault Marine and Raptor: tapping Space while falling (off a ledge, at the top of a jump or on the way down) now does the short hop in the air. Holding Space while falling still jumps again as soon as you land.
- Assault Marine, Raptor and Tactical: a charge that runs off a ledge can now be steered a little left and right with A and D while it flies, at the same speed.
- True Grit: the Bolter and Relic Bolter in the pistol slot now hang down at the side at rest, like the bolt pistol, instead of being held out level, and walk, run, sprint, raise, shoot and reload one-handed like the bolt pistol (seen by you and by other players). Their first shot from the lowered gun now comes as quickly as the bolt pistol's.
- Mouse sensitivity slider: a new "Mouse" slider (0.10x to 3.00x, 1.00x is the old feel) sits next to the FOV slider on the Join screen and in the in-match menu (Esc, then the loadout menu) in every mode. It changes how far the view turns per mouse movement, in hip fire, aimed and scoped alike, takes effect at once and is remembered. The controller is not affected.
- Camera: a roll, hop or jump pack dash while looking down keeps your character in view, instead of the camera dropping behind you and losing him.
- Camera at walls: backing into a wall keeps the camera over your shoulder instead of inside the head, with a pillar tight on your right it moves over your left shoulder, it no longer pumps in and out at pillars and door frames, and it eases back out more gently. The crosshair stays on the same spot while the camera is pulled in. Check how it feels at walls, corners and pillars.
- Helmet lens colour: the colour you pick in the customizer (EYES) is now the colour of the lens itself, on the helmet, for you and for other players, in the preview and in the match. The glow that used to come with it is gone (no glow around the eyes and no tint on the Chaos weapon eye glow, which keeps its own colours). The colours on offer are the same. The Chaos berserk helmets with the black visor slit have no lens and do not change.
- Musou Mode: the run no longer ends if you are short of supplies after a won battle. If you go down after winning, the extraction still happens, it takes what supplies are left and the run goes on. A player who joins during a battle now sees the normal respawn countdown instead of KILLED.
- Musou Mode can now be played from start to end of a battle. In the Stormbird walk to a console and press E: Destination opens the war map with the three fronts and DEPLOY, Equipment opens your loadouts. Hold M at any time to see the war map. The screen shows the fronts, the HQ, transport, supplies and war time, and in battle the objectives, your Guardsmen and your retainer. In battle Z makes your retainer follow, X holds it where you aim, V sends it at what you aim at (in this mode X and V do only that: use the mouse wheel and the right mouse button to switch weapons and strike), and holding P for a second calls a pickup. If you fall, your retainer comes to revive you; without it you are extracted for 2 supplies. Tell us what is unclear.
- New menus everywhere: a clean main menu with PLAYER VERSUS PLAYER, MUSOU MODE, OPTIONS, CUSTOMIZER and QUIT. Hosting now has ADD LOYALIST BOT and ADD CHAOS BOT. JOIN SERVER goes straight to the public server, JOIN LOCAL LOBBY takes an address. OPTIONS has graphics, sound and controls settings (resolution, window mode, FPS cap, render scale, volumes, invert Y and more); the FOV and mouse sliders and the keybinds moved there. Esc in a match shows the same menu over the game, with your loadouts under LOADOUT. Try them all and tell us what looks wrong or does nothing.
- Heavier actions: landings from a drop now crouch and dip the camera by how far you fell, the ground pound and nearby explosions jolt the camera a little more, heavy weapons kick the view and the body harder (try the lascannon and the heavy bolter), targets flinch harder when shot or hit in melee, and melee swings and hit reactions start faster. Devastators and Havocs feel their steps a little in the camera. Tell us what is too much or too little.
- Devastator and Havoc backs: the Havoc no longer wears a Space Marine backpack along with its own, the Devastator's grey box is replaced by the pack that goes with its heavy weapon, and the Devastator now shows the Iron Halo on top of its pack when it has the perk.
- Roll, Devastator and Havoc hop, Assault Marine and Raptor short hop: these now have the original game's cooldown of 0.8 seconds, up from about 0.56, so Space can no longer be mashed to chain them as fast. A press during the cooldown does nothing: no move, no animation, and it does not break you out of a stun or a charge. A press in the last moment before the cooldown ends still goes through, and holding Space no longer rolls again on its own. Assault Marine and Raptor can still hold Space to jump during the cooldown. Tell us if it still feels too quick compared with the original.
- Musou Mode now starts its own server on your PC the first time you press MUSOU MODE (this PC only, nothing is opened to the network) and joins it; pressing it again while the server is still starting waits instead of failing, and later presses join the running server. While that server runs, STOP MUSOU SERVER appears under MUSOU MODE on the main menu and stops it.
- Freezes no longer drop you from the match: if the game stops responding for several seconds (for example the first time an effect is drawn on a slow graphics card, which showed up as being thrown out of the server when a hit landed), you now stay connected and carry on when it comes back, for up to 30 seconds. Your character stands still while the game is frozen. If you are ever dropped, the game's log now says whether the game froze or the server went silent.
- F11 switches fullscreen on and off anywhere: in the menus, in the Options screen (its Window mode row follows at once) and in a match, with the customizer or loadout picker open. It is remembered like the Window mode setting, and the mouse stays captured in a match.
- Thunder Hammer and Daemon Maul (Assault Marine and Raptor): the three standard swings step forward about 1.5 m less than before (the hit still lands on a target you are locked to), and your legs now travel with the lunge through the swings and into the next strike in the combo instead of staying planted and stretching out behind you. The air slash (jump, then melee) with the hammer or the maul now plays the hammer's own landing animation instead of the sword's; damage, stagger and effects are the same.
- Musou Mode: the orbital strike has no cooldown any more. Every press of B in a battle calls a strike, even while another one is still falling. The shells still hurt you and your allies.
- Musou Mode: the sound of aircraft flying past is gone from the distant war.
- Power weapons now flash on every hit: the thunder hammer, power sword and power axe burst into a big blue-white cloud where they strike, and the daemon maul (Chaos) bursts purple, in normal matches and in Musou Mode. The hammer and maul also boom with the hammer's own blast sound. A swing through a crowd shows one big burst and a few small ones, and the burst shrinks if your view is right inside it so you can still see.
- New hit sounds for melee weapons: the power axe, power sword and combat knife now hit with a short electric sizzle; the chainsword, thunder hammer, daemon maul and the Tactical's and Assault's charge (F) hit with a longer crackling roar. Repeats vary a little so they never sound identical. These are new, made-up sounds that nobody has heard yet, so tell us how they sound.
- Blood is darker and there is much more of it: every hit on an Ork in Musou Mode now throws a big brown-red cloud and black ink splatter, kills and explosions make huge hanging banks of it, and the floor stays stained. The same blood now shows in normal player-versus-player matches when you play Extended (Faithful has none).
- Musou Mode: the orbital strike now kills every enemy its blast touches except bosses, which take only minor damage, in a 12 metre ball around each explosion, at any height. Your own side still takes damage.
- Musou Mode: the Orks now gather in crowds instead of trickling at you. Each of the three points on the map (A, B and C, the three you capture) builds a crowd of 50 Orks that stands still on the point, one more every second, and when it reaches 50 the whole crowd charges you at once; the point then starts a new crowd, and each point swarms on its own. The 150 Orks of the three crowds are the most alive at once, so a crowd refills as its swarm is killed and you get quiet between swarms to recover. Each point also has a garrison of 12 Orks that defends it from the start and never comes back once killed. Walk close to a crowd, or shoot it, and it charges early with the Orks it has so far. As before, a point you are capturing stops adding Orks while you are on it and a point you have captured stops for good. Tell us how long the quiet between swarms feels and whether 50 Orks a point is too many or too few.
- New Musou Mode rules version: this build does not join Musou servers of earlier builds.
- Musou Mode: every capture point now has a name in the world and on the screen: a large "A", "B  DEPOT" or "C" with its state (HELD, CLEARING, SECURING 42%, SECURED) floats over its ring in the colour of the ring and shows through walls, and the list at the top right marks the one to take next with "< NEXT". To capture a point you stand on it with no Ork left alive inside its ring (about 15 metres wide and 10 metres up and down), so Orks standing at the edge, on the floor below or lying knocked down still count: kill them or wait for them to come. Tell us if a point still refuses to capture and what was standing near it.
- Musou Mode: an Ork stuck standing on a point you are clearing (on a ledge it cannot leave, or hovering at the edge) is removed after a few seconds, so a point can always be captured once you have beaten the Orks that come at you. A crowd now also wakes when you stand at the edge of its point.
- Musou Mode: when a crowd hits you, the screen no longer strobes yellow and red. A hit while the flash is still showing now keeps it steady instead of flashing it up to full again each time; a single hit still flashes as before. The far-off explosions around the arena no longer light the whole battlefield with a cream glow for a moment.
- Light melee attacks, the combat knife included, no longer stagger, stun or interrupt anything: a charge, hop, swing or reload carries on. They only throw off the aim of a player who is shooting, for half a second. Stun strikes, charges, stomps and kicks still stagger and stun.
- Musou Mode: enemies drop ammunition and equipment boxes, crates around the map hold weapons for your class, and after each cleared map you choose a new weapon, piece of equipment or perk. Start a run with any loadout; new weapons go in slots 3, 4 and 5, on those number keys.
- Musou Mode: every map and run now has a score built from your chain bonus, with your best kept and a COPY RESULT button to share it.
- Musou Mode: press B to call an orbital strike on the spot under your crosshair. Shells whistle in about a second later.
- Musou Mode: distant war sounds now surround the battlefield.
- Musou Mode: the team score panel at the bottom left is gone.
