- Musou Mode: more effects can stay on screen in big fights (the limit is more than twice as high), and when the screen is full the effects that matter to you stay: your own muzzle flashes, tracers and impacts, hits on you and by you, then bosses and special attacks, then your allies, and the far-off crowd's gunfire is what disappears first.
- Bolter, stalker bolter and the other bolt weapons no longer leave black smoke clouds and dark flying chunks, in Classic PvP and in Musou Mode: the flash, sparks and bullet marks stay.
- Musou Mode: the autocannon throws no dust or grit any more, only the bright yellow explosive flash, and its rounds flash where they hit the ground, a wall or a prop even when no Ork is hit.
- Musou Mode, the Dreadnought is stronger. His arm swings 30% faster and every Ork inside the swing is hit; the swings now flow smoothly from one into the next instead of popping. His cannon fires like the Devastator's autocannon (same damage, explosions, sounds and flash) but ten shots a second, never runs out of ammunition, and has a heat bar: hold fire for about 5 seconds and it overheats, then cools. His melta is a bigger Tactical melta: 1.5 times the damage, longer reach, full damage all the way out, a faster reload and no running out of fuel. Try them all in a war as the Dreadnought and tell us how the heat bar and the melta's reach feel.
- Leaving a match now returns cleanly to the main menu: no leftover loadout or spawn prompts, no HUD, no game sounds and no "Disconnected" box after you leave, also when you hosted the match yourself.
- Musou Mode, the Dreadnought's power-up now has Overcharge for its 30 seconds: the cannon builds no heat and fires without stopping, and every melee swing that connects releases a small shockwave that knocks back and interrupts the Orks around the hit.
- Musou Mode, the Dreadnought is now the Dreadnought Assault Dreadnought: his screen shake (footfalls, stomps, landing, arrival, death), arrival and death effects, damage effects, engine, cannon sounds and muzzle flash are the original's; he runs and sprints 30% faster. The shake follows the Camera Shake and Reduce Motion settings.
