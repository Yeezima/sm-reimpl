- Tapping Space as an Assault Marine or a Raptor now plays a single jump-pack hop sound. The extra roll-style whoosh and the doubled jump-pack burst that came with it are gone, for you and for the players you hear, in every mode.
- Hopping out of a charge that runs off a ledge (Assault Marine and Raptor; Classic PvP and Musou Mode): tap Space, with or without a direction, while falling in the charge and you no longer stop almost dead; you keep the charge's speed and ease down smoothly to the hop's top speed. A charge off a ledge now shows its charge pose for 0.4 seconds, then blends into the falling pose, and lands normally if you do nothing else. This build does not play with earlier ones: everyone must update.
