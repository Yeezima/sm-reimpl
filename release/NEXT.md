- A new network version: everyone, players and servers, must update to this build; older builds cannot join.

### Classic PvP
- The autocannon is as accurate as the stalker bolter at range: tight shots, small spread.
- The autocannon's firing sound is louder and punchier instead of washed out.
- Allies show their eagle and name above their head whenever they are on your screen, at any distance and through walls.
- Chaos plasma guns, plasma pistols and plasma cannons fire in Chaos magenta-purple, matching their muzzle flash, instead of the loyalist blue.
- Manufactorum: the last out-of-place salmon-red glowing pipes, tanks and panels are gone.
- Power sword: F + F now ends in a sword thrust that hits like a Furious Charge bull rush, instead of the stomp.

### Both modes
- Assault Marine and Raptor: flight looks smoother, with steadier poses in the air and landings that start just before touchdown.
- Assault Marine and Raptor: if you jump (hold Space) after charging off a ledge you now do a full jump out of the charge, instead of just dropping; a jump pressed just before the edge of a charge lifts off too.
- Assault Marine and Raptor: a hop after a light melee swing shortens the wait before your next swing, so swing, hop, swing flows faster; the wait is cut to about half and never to nothing, so you cannot spam swings by hopping.
- Assault Marine and Raptor: pressing hop in the middle of an aerial (flying) tackle cancels the tackle straight into a hop.
- Assault Marine and Raptor: every melee weapon can ground pound as soon as you are high enough in a jump, even while still rising.
- Devastator and Havoc: after a stomp you can act again 1.2 seconds in, so you can fire your bolt pistol right after the stomp lands.
- Sparks from a hit no longer fly more than 4 m from the player who was shot, and the red and yellow hit flashes are 40% stronger.
- Combat stimulants show the original game's glow and icon above the player.
- Tactical Marine: no weapon flies off during an F + F stun strike, with or without Weapon Versatility.
- Power weapons cast the thunder hammer's blue light; with the Serrated Combat Blade perk the combat knife glows green.
- Kraken Bolts give the bolter large, thick red tracers.
- Press F7 in a match for a sound mixer: turn up or down your own actions, each kind of gun, footsteps, explosions and more, and how sounds fade with distance. Your settings are saved.
- The main menu shows the forge-world city picture again.

### Musou Mode
- The Thunderhawk troop bay: the coloured floor circles are replaced by an armoury, a firing-range airlock and a deployment bulkhead on the walls; walk up to one and press E.
