- Warfront: a retainer's jump pack (Assault and Raptor squads) is now painted like the backpack colours you picked in the customizer, instead of the side's default paint.
- Warfront: your Marine retainers now shoot like the practice bots but miss far less, stick to the Ork they are fighting for about a second instead of flipping targets, favour Orks that attack you or a downed squad-mate, and grow tougher and hit harder as your level rises. Everyone must update: Warfront games from older builds will not match.
- Mapmaker: the Map tab has a new Grid section (below Snap). Tick Show grid, pick the grid's colour, set the cell size in metres and the height (or press From selection to put it at the bottom of the selected object), and tick Draw over objects to see the grid through pieces. The grid never changes when you zoom, is saved with your map and does not change how the map plays.
- Mapmaker: importing an original map now brings Classic's invisible walls, kill boxes, shoot-through cover and the objects Classic hides (switched off, you can turn them back on), and sets the original look for the map.
