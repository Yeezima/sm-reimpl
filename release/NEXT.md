- New network version: everyone must update to this build to play together, and the public server is updated to match.
### Classic PvP and everywhere
- Assault and Raptor: press Jump to break out of any stun, hit reaction or attack animation. A jump cancelled by a melee attack now keeps its sideways momentum but loses its upward speed.
- Classic PvP jump pack: fuel refills during a jump's hang and fall, so two full jumps back to back work like the original.
- The bolt pistol fires straight away after switching to it from the lascannon; every weapon keeps its own shot cooldown.
- The grenade throw animation plays out longer. Plasma charged shots fire the moment you release the button.
- Thunder hammer and power maul: the light combo recovers about a second sooner.
- Bots in Classic PvP miss melee now and then, strafe in gunfights, flank when a teammate engages from the front, and no longer get free headshots while airborne.
- While dead and waiting to respawn you can orbit the camera around your body.
- Bolt tracers fly in a straight line from the barrel to where the round hits. Plasma shots are a slim blue bolt, and the charged shot's scorch mark appears when the shot arrives.
- The plasma crosshair no longer spins while charging.
- Customizer: ten saved appearance slots per side that you can apply to any loadout.
- Sound mix pass and reload sounds.
- Light sword strikes no longer make the legs stretch.
### Musou Mode
- Bolt weapons set off a small explosion at every enemy their round passes through.
- Ork kills now show the original game's blood: a red flash, dark streaks, a crimson cloud and floor splats, half again bigger and heavier.
- The Warboss now has his full body and head and stands half again taller.
- New enemy: the Weirdboy, with a shield, warp bolts, teleports and summons. Breaking his shield knocks him down; his death burst hurts other Orks but spares the Warboss and Boss Orks.
- Fallen players can redeploy at the landing zone for 1 supply. The host can call everyone back to the Stormbird: survive 20 seconds, then you leave.
- After the Warboss falls, a giant Ork wave comes from every side.
- Killing a big crowd with a grenade no longer freezes the game.
- Ashfall has a new look: a storm sky, darker and dirtier buildings, light beams, smoke and embers. The layout is cleaned up: no stacked objects, open stair bottoms, fixed stair collision and new rubble piles.
