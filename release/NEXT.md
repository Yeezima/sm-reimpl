- New network version: everyone must update to this build to play together, and the public servers are updated to match.
- Classic PvP: the score panel at the bottom left now matches the original's layout: the thin gold frame, the original font, the team emblem, and in Seize Ground a marker per point that blinks while it is being taken.
- Classic PvP: the power sword's stab now has its own key, T, and T no longer plays a voice call.
- Classic PvP: the lascannon fires a shot that is already due before a jump starts, instead of losing it to the hop.
- Plasma cannon: holding the charged shot fires it by itself at full charge. The charged shot has a soft glowing trail with faint violet traces and a slightly larger bright ball at the front, and its blast has a softer edge with no hard ring.
- Grenades: your own frag grenade in flight, and enemy frag grenades near you, carry an orange marker; Master-Crafted grenades look and sound bigger; frag blasts leave light smoke and give a short camera blur up close.
- Classic PvP bots: they fall back when badly hurt and return after a few seconds, hunt down players instead of wandering, keep out of the enemy spawn, and move better on the newer maps.
- Classic maps: skies now show properly, glowing signs and lights are no longer darkened, and a missing floor on one map is fixed.
- Musou Mode: the Orks near you (up to 100) flinch with the game's own hit reactions, and each Ork picks its own flinch, throw and death animation.
- Musou Mode, the Dreadnought: his cannon fires where the crosshair points, his swings cleave 50% wider, he can fire right after a swing, his hits and sustained fire shake the screen, and he no longer gets the red damage flash.
- Musou Mode: Marines from a drop pod patrol up to 60 metres around the pod when they have nothing to shoot.
- Musou Mode: chain lightning no longer lifts or knocks Orks back, and its first jump reaches further.
