### Both modes
- More of the original's own movement animations now play: a quick half-speed stop when you let go of sprint soon after starting it, a 180-degree pivot when you sprint off in the direction behind you, and the thunder hammer's own stop out of a sprint.
- Pressing the equipment key with combat stims now uses them far more reliably in every mode, Classic matches too: the press is remembered for half a second while a swing or a charge finishes, and a hit in the first moment of the use no longer cancels it.
- This build does not play with earlier ones (a new network version): everyone must update before joining a game.

### Musou Mode
- In the customizer's 3D view the new weapons now show in the hands: choose the autocannon (Musou Mode, and Classic with the extended content) and the figure stands ready with it held across the body; the lightning claw and the power spear are shown in the match's ready stance too. Original weapons look as before.
- A new SUPPORT step in the customizer (Musou Mode's pages: the marines' loadouts and the Librarian) chooses what B calls, one choice for each loadout. The first is the artillery strike, as before. The second is a drop pod assault: B marks a point with the laser, a few seconds later a drop pod falls from the sky in fire and smoke and shakes the ground, its doors drop open and four Tactical Marines fight for 30 seconds, then retreat to the Imperial base and vanish. One pod at a time, and the same one-minute wait as the artillery strike. This build does not play with earlier ones (a new network version): everyone must update.
- The made-up background yelling is gone (the distant Ork mobs shouting and the armies' war cries); only Orks and Marines actually on the field make noise, and their voices now carry about three quarters farther, so you hear everyone from further away.
- Melee attacks reach 20 percent further for every weapon, including the power spear's sweeps.
- The Librarian can now zoom in with the aim button like every other class (the same zoom, the same speed). He can still cast while zoomed.
- The autocannon's barrel now joins the body of the gun without a gap where it begins to stick out.
- The drop pod assault now crushes every Ork within 30 metres where it lands (three times an artillery strike's damage), the customizer's step is called SUPPORT ABILITIES, and the four Marines who step out each shout FOR THE EMPEROR a moment apart, never all at once. Everyone has to update (a new network version).
- Ork bosses and the Warboss hit harder (about half again), though never hard enough to kill a full-health marine in one hit. A boss is now stunned for 5 seconds each time it drops to 80, 60, 40 and 20 percent health, so those are your windows to hit it. Boss Orks arrive about twice as often, up to 12 at a time. Power-ups still drop only from elites and bosses.
- The lightning claw is now part of the Assault Marine's gauntlet instead of a weapon held in the fist: a dark armoured housing with brass sockets, pistons and a vented power dome on the back of the hand, and four long, slightly curved steel blades running out past the fingers, fixed to the hand in every move.
- The lightning claw now crackles with power: its blades glow a strong blue-white with a halo, thin jagged lightning arcs crawl along them and jump between them (more on every strike), and its armoured housing is weathered like the rest of your armour and takes your gauntlet's colours.
- Musou Mode: the Assault and Raptor no longer do the flying tackle (it is switched off for now); a melee press at any moment of a hop starts a normal light attack at once, with no wait before the next one. Musou Mode servers and players must update together.
- This build does not play with earlier ones (new network version): everyone must update.
- Both modes: you can charge (Shift + W + F) right after a melee attack, a light combo or a heavy strike once the swing has connected, and swing again straight after a charge ends or is cancelled.
- Both modes: tapping S (or pulling the stick back) cancels a charge at once, from the first moment of the charge, with no half-second wait.
- Both modes: a light melee press (right click) during a charge, even on its very last moment, cancels the charge and swings at once. A melee attack between two charges lifts the one second wait, so charge, melee, charge, melee chains with no delay (a charge cancelled with S and repeated within a second still waits).
- Musou Mode: light melee attacks can be spammed with no cooldown or delay.
- The weapon and equipment panel now stands in the bottom right corner, the same distance from the screen's edges as your health and armour bar is from the bottom left, and the health and armour bar is 10 percent larger. The support call (B) and the Librarian's Shockwave show as cooldown cells in a row beside the equipment panel, in its style: an icon (the artillery burst, the drop pod, the Shockwave) and the seconds left or READY, with the cooldown bar beneath each cell.
- The power spear's animations have had another pass: standing still he now carries it as in your reference (hips square to you, the right arm hanging beside the hip, the blade pointing forward and up), the three light hews, the F snap cut and the F, F stab have a short compact wind-up, speed up into the blow, hold the finish only a moment and recover quickly, and the hews flow from the carry and into each other without popping; running and sprinting keep the older chest-height carry for now, because a hip-height hand put the shaft through his legs.
- The power spear is now carried the way you asked: one-handed in the right hand at the hip, the shaft running along his spine (it leans with him as he runs and sprints, blade up, butt low, tipped a little inward so the blade stays out of the crosshair), the right arm swinging slightly and the left freely, the hand on the shaft with the arm connected to it; the hews, the snap cut and the stab are as you liked them. A spear or armour passing through the body for a moment no longer counts as a fault.
- The power spear's hands got another pass: standing, his left hand is now the bolt pistol idle's own; running, both arms pump against the stride (the left one bent at the elbow, rising as it comes forward, the spear hand carrying the shaft with a small swing) and the right hand closes its fingers round the shaft; the three light hews, the F snap cut and the F, F stab have a more compact wind-up, accelerate harder into the blow and carry the hips through after it. The slight tilt that keeps the blade off the crosshair is unchanged.
- The power spear's hands got a second pass: the hand now grips the shaft finger by finger (no finger through it, the wrist turned so the shaft crosses the palm), the left hand reaches the shaft when it takes it, the running left arm no longer flickers at the elbow, the carry leans a little forward, and the three hews, the snap cut and the stab have a tighter wind-up and more weight in the follow-through.
- The Librarian's chain lightning now leaps twice as far from Ork to Ork (up to 16 m) and no longer weakens down the chain: every Ork it strikes, the last as the first, takes the full damage.
- Orbital strikes you call now do 20 % more damage to the Orks too, which bosses feel most (ordinary Orks die in the blast either way).
- In Musou Mode too, the grenade key no longer cuts a charge short; tap or hold S instead (charging into a teammate slides past him there too).
- The autocannon is much stronger: each round's explosion now kills any ordinary Ork (Boyz, Shoota Boyz, Gretchin, Rokkit Boyz, Stormboyz, Squigs) inside its blast that it reaches (not behind cover or a Mek's shield), and the round itself hits even harder (30 more than the blast). Its ammunition never runs out (an infinity sign in the ammo box); it still overheats after 15 rounds.
- The autocannon is much stronger: each round's explosion now kills any ordinary Ork (Boyz, Shoota Boyz, Gretchin, Rokkit Boyz, Squigs) inside its blast that it reaches (not behind cover or a Mek's shield), and the round itself hits even harder (30 more than the blast). Stormboyz are tougher than that: one explosion leaves them hurt but standing.
- The autocannon has no magazine: it never reloads and keeps firing as long as you hold the trigger (an infinity sign in the ammo box). It still overheats after 15 rounds, so watch the heat bar.
- No gun loses damage with distance any more: every shot from every gun (bolters, Stalker Bolter, lascannon, plasma, the autocannon and the rest) hits as hard at long range as up close, and the melta burns at full strength to the end of its reach. Grenades and other explosions still hurt less towards the edge of the blast.
- The Devastator now wears the heavy bolter's backpack when carrying the autocannon.
- Musou Mode in this build does not play with earlier builds: everyone in a Musou match, and the server, needs this update.

### Deaths and hits (both modes)
- An Assault Marine or Raptor shot down in the middle of a jump-pack jump now plays the original's mid-air death: the body jerks from the hit as the pack bursts into flame, tumbles down head first, slams into the ground with a crater and smoke, and only then goes limp. Shoot one out of the air to see it.
