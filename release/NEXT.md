- Assault Marines and Raptors with a chainsword or a thunder hammer now fall and land with their own animations from the original game: dropping off a ledge of a metre or more plays the melee fall, then a short landing. The thunder hammer also has its own jump-pack landings, both standing and running.

### Musou Mode
- The drop pod assault now brings 5 Tactical Marines (was 4). When their 30 seconds are up they fall back to the Imperial base instead of running into the Ork stronghold.
- The Ork Warboss and the Boss Orks no longer go into a stun at 80, 60, 40 and 20 percent health. Grenades, blasts, the artillery strike, the drop pod and the Librarian's powers still cannot stun them, but your stunning melee attacks now do: heavy attacks, charge hits and the hammer's ground pound each stun a boss briefly. The AI Marines' melee cannot. Musou Mode servers and players must update together.
