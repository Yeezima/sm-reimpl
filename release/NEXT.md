- Musou Mode changed its rules and its map: update to this build to join a Musou Mode server.

### Musou Mode
- New Ashfall Citadel layout: large buildings with strong high ground. Devastators and Tacticals get rooftops and terraces to fight from, and the Assault Marine's jump pack reaches them. Every stair leads somewhere and no doorway is walled off.
- Stunned bosses no longer spin around: they stand dizzy in place, swaying, with stars circling above their head until the stun ends.
- The red and blue melee slash arcs now follow the path of the weapon's tip through the swing, still drawn out at the reach of the cleave.
- Drop Pod Assault: call a new pod as soon as the 60 second cooldown ends, even while the last squad is still fighting.
- The Assault Marine's ground pound and air-slash landing cover half again as much ground and hit 50% harder at the centre.
- Every weapon's blast is 25% wider; the autocannon's burst is 40% wider.
- A melee swing strikes every Ork inside its cleave, however many there are, and up to five of them heal you (was three).
